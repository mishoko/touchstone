Uniswap v4 hook vault

Touchstone

A Uniswap v4 hook vault in which the pool keeps the curator's books.

Your curator pays for its own trades, as the pool measures them.

How a charge works

  1. Curator acts
  2. Vault valued
  3. Shortfall burned
  4. Uncovered reverts

  1. The curator acts.

    It places liquidity or swaps inside the vault.

  2. The pool values the vault.

    It takes the worse of spot and the reference TWAP.

    The reference is a deep pool fixed at deployment.

  3. The shortfall is burned.

    It comes from the curator's unvested pay first.

    Then it comes from the curator's bond.

  4. An action they cannot cover reverts.

    The trade does not happen.
