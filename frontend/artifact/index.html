The pool keeps the curator's books.

A liquidity vault on Uniswap v4. Every curator action is priced against a reference price. The cost the pool measures is charged to the curator's deferred pay, then its bond. An action it cannot cover reverts.

How it works

Five rules. One price to check against.

The numbers below are the mainnet tier's. The demo deployment runs the same rules faster (table below).

  1. A reference price

    The 10-minute TWAP of a deep Uniswap v3 pool. Fixed at deployment.

  2. A fence

    An action more than 60 ticks from the reference reverts.

    test_U_guard__fenceOnBothSides

  3. A charge

    The vault is valued before and after, at three prices. The worst shortfall is charged, 100%. Pay first, then bond. Not enough? Revert.

    test_U_solv__worstSwapPlusReserveFitsTheBond

  4. A settlement

    A reserve is held. 120 s later the action is settled against the reference. The lag gap is burned, up to the reserve.

  5. A mandate

    A 2,000-tick range holding spot and the reference. Reference outside it: no base fee, no vesting. 24 h out: a breach. Three: the curator is removed.

  • Pay stops the moment the reference leaves the range.
  • Unvested pay is forfeited to depositors at the first breach.
  • Three breaches remove the curator. There is no replacement curator.
  • Withdrawals stay open in every mode.

For depositors

What you get. What you do not.

Benefits

  • The curator pays for its own trades, as the pool measures them. The measured cost comes out of the curator's pay and bond. U-3 · E2
  • Other traders are never charged to you. 0 wei charged over 10,000 segments and 20,034 third-party swaps, in the independent model. E3
  • A curator who walks away pays you. Its unvested pay is forfeited to depositors at the first breach.
  • The base fee is paid for work in range. It accrues only while the mandate liquidity is in range.
  • You can always leave. Withdrawals are in kind, in every mode.

Limits

  • Market moves are yours. Positioning and token mix are not covered. Skill is not rewarded. The reference TWAP is assumed not to be moved; moving it costs money (E8).
  • Lag leak. Prices lag the market by a block. The settlement closes the gap in expectation, not on every draw. The reserve caps one settlement. E8
  • Reported, not asserted. Some attack rows net a positive amount: +0.93 (a markout past the cap, about 8e-4 of NAV), +0.004 (protocol fee on), +0.0006 (off-leg stops), +0.00023 per exit (parking, test tier; about 5e-6 of NAV on mainnet). Token units of a 1,150-NAV test vault. E5
  • One block builder. A single builder that censors arbitrage is outside the model. Against it the safe NAV is about 150 WETH, not 1,850.
  • The NAV cap is checked at the last action. A vault left unplaced through a large move can hold more than the cap.
  • Incentives are simulated. U-IC2 passed on synthetic paths without an outside observer. With one, two lax strategies out-earn the reference curator at 120% volatility. The vault ends with more value under those lax strategies (about 558 and 569 against 314, from 1,000). U-IC2
  • E-IC was not run. No historical price path was fetched. E-IC
  • Allocation ledger claim withdrawn. E0b failed: 59 of 112 epoch rows exceeded 0.3 bp. The ledger is disclosure only. E0b
  • Synthetic paths only. Every simulated result uses simulated 180-day paths.
  • Testnet only. No third-party review. The tests, evaluations and SECURITY.md are the evidence. The testnet reference is our own mock pool.
